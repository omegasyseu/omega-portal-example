# Privacy Policy

## {{brand}}

This Privacy Policy describes how {{brand}} collects, uses and handles your personal data when you use this website and its services.

## What & Why

We collect and use the following information to provide, improve, protect, and promote our Services.

## Account Information

We collect and associate with your account the information you provide to us when you register, update your address, or set up two-factor authentication. This includes your name, email address, phone number, payment info, and physical address.

## Documents You Provide

When you upload documents to verify your identity or address, or send us files through customer support, we store and process them along with related information such as the upload time.

## Usage Information

We collect information about how you use the Services, including your logins, deposits, withdrawals, bets and the limits you set. This helps us improve and protect the Services.

## Device Information

We collect information from devices you use to access the Services, such as IP addresses, browser type, and location (depending on settings).

## Cookies and Other Technologies

We use cookies to provide, improve, and promote our Services. You can learn more about cookies and manage your preferences through your browser or opt-out of advertising cookies via third-party services.

You are told about cookies on your first visit. Essential cookies keep you signed in and cannot be switched off.

## Marketing

We send marketing only if you agreed to receive it. You can opt out of marketing communications via the ‘unsubscribe’ link in emails or by updating preferences in your account settings.

## With Whom

We may share information with trusted third parties to provide, improve, and protect our Services. These parties are bound by our instructions and policies.

We may disclose your information if required by law, to protect individuals from harm, or to perform tasks in the public interest.

## How

We implement security measures like encryption, two-factor authentication, and device alerts to keep your information secure. You can review and update your details in your account.

Data retention policies ensure information is kept only as long as necessary to provide our Services or meet legal obligations.

## Where

Your data may be stored and processed in the jurisdiction in which {{brand}} is licensed or in other locations, in compliance with applicable laws and guidelines.

## How long we keep your data

We keep your account and transaction records for as long as your account is open and afterwards for the period the law and our licence require, then delete or anonymise them.

## How we protect your data

Your data is encrypted in transit, access to it is restricted to staff who need it, and access is logged.

## Your Control and Access of Your Data

- Self-exclude or close your account via account settings.
- Request your data in a downloadable CSV format.
- Edit or delete your personal information through account settings.
- Object to data processing by contacting us at [info@omegasys.eu](mailto:info@omegasys.eu).

## Changes

If involved in a reorganization, merger, or sale, your data may be transferred. We will notify you of such events and provide options.

## Contact

Have questions or concerns? Contact our Data Protection Officer at [info@omegasys.eu](mailto:info@omegasys.eu). If they can’t answer your question, you have the right to contact your local data protection supervisory authority.
