# Gaming Rules

These rules apply to every game offered by {{brand}}. They sit alongside our [Terms and Conditions](/legal/terms). Where a game's own rules differ from these, the game's own rules apply to that game.

## Who can play

- You must be of legal gambling age in your place of residence, and at least 18, to open an account and play.
- You may hold only one account. Play is only allowed from an account registered and verified in your own name.
- You must not play from a place where online gambling is illegal.

## Each game's own rules and return to player

- Every game has its own rules, paytable and theoretical return to player (RTP). Open them from the help or information button inside the game before you play.
- The RTP is a long-term theoretical average across all players. It does not predict the result of any session.
- The result of every game round is decided by the game's certified random number generator. Earlier results do not affect later ones.

## Bets and settlement

- A bet is placed when the game accepts it. Your balance is reduced by the stake at that moment.
- Winnings are credited to your balance when the round ends, as shown in the game.
- Your bets, wins and balance changes are recorded in your Gaming History and Transaction History.
- Minimum and maximum stakes are shown in each game.

## Interrupted games

- If your connection drops during a round, the round is completed on the game server. Reopen the game to see the result. Any win is credited to your balance.
- An unfinished round that cannot be completed is settled according to that game's rules.

## Malfunction

- A malfunction voids all pays and plays. If a game or our systems malfunction, the affected bets and wins are void and the stakes of the voided bets are returned to your balance.

## Voided bets

We may void a bet if it was placed:
- in breach of these rules or our Terms and Conditions;
- through an obvious technical error, such as a wrong price or paytable;
- by someone other than the account holder, or by an underage person.

## Bonuses

Bonuses have their own wagering requirements and expiry, shown with each bonus and in our Terms and Conditions.

## Questions

If you think a game result or settlement is wrong, see [Complaints and Contact](/legal/complaints).
