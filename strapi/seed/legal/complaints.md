# Complaints and Contact

## Contact us

Our support team can help with your account, payments, games, limits and self-exclusion.

- **Email**: support@omegasys.eu

Please include your username, and never send us your password.

## Making a complaint

1. Email your complaint to support@omegasys.eu with your username, the date and time of the event, and what happened. Include the game name or transaction reference if relevant.
2. We acknowledge your complaint within 2 business days.
3. We investigate and send you our final response within 14 days. If we need more time, we tell you why and when to expect it.

Our records of your bets and transactions are used to investigate. You can see them in your Gaming History and Transaction History.

## If you are not satisfied

If you are not satisfied with our final response, you can refer your complaint, free of charge, to the independent dispute resolution body or the gambling regulator that licenses {{brand}}. Their details are given in our final response.
