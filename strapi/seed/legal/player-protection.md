# Player Protection Information

{{brand}} wants gambling to stay a form of entertainment. This page explains the risks, the tools you can use to stay in control, and where to get help.

## The risks of gambling

- Gambling can lead to losing more money or time than you intended.
- Never gamble to recover losses, to earn income, or when you are upset, tired or under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Only gamble with money you can afford to lose.

## Signs that gambling may be a problem

- Spending more money or time than you planned, or hiding how much you gamble.
- Chasing losses, or borrowing money to gamble.
- Gambling affecting your work, studies, relationships or health.
- Feeling anxious or irritable when you are not gambling.

If any of these apply to you, use the tools below and consider contacting one of the help organisations listed on this page.

## Tools to stay in control

You can set these yourself, at any time, on the [Responsible Gaming](/responsible-gaming) page once you are signed in:

- **Deposit limit**: caps how much you can deposit each day, week or month.
- **Wager limit**: caps how much you can bet each day, week or month.
- **Loss limit**: caps how much you can lose each day, week or month.
- **Session limit**: caps how long you can play in one session.
- **Reality check**: reminds you at a set interval how long you have been playing.
- **Self-exclusion**: closes your account to gambling for a period you choose, or permanently.

Lowering a limit takes effect at once. Raising or removing a limit only takes effect after a waiting period, so the decision is not made in the moment.

While you are self-excluded you cannot sign in to play or deposit, and you will not receive marketing from us. Self-exclusion cannot be reversed before its end date.

## Your account history

Your Gaming History and Transaction History, available when you are signed in, show every bet, win, deposit and withdrawal, so you can always see how much you have spent.

## Underage gambling

Gambling by anyone under 18, or under the legal age where they live, is prohibited. We verify the age and identity of every player. If you share a device with a minor, keep your password secret and consider filtering software.

## Where to get help

Free and confidential support is available:

- **GamCare**: www.gamcare.org.uk
- **Gamblers Anonymous**: www.gamblersanonymous.org
- **BeGambleAware**: www.begambleaware.org

You can also contact our support team, who can help you set limits or self-exclude. See [Complaints and Contact](/legal/complaints).
